# RegOS

**RegOS is a knowledge platform for regulatory affairs.**

It transforms regulations, evidence, products, and submissions into structured knowledge that enables explainable regulatory decisions.

RegOS is not built around documents.

It is built around facts, rules, decisions, and knowledge.

---

# Vision

To become the operating system for regulatory affairs by enabling every regulatory decision to be made from trusted knowledge.

---

# What is RegOS?

RegOS is a platform designed for regulated industries such as medical devices, pharmaceuticals, and in vitro diagnostics.

Instead of treating regulatory work as document management, RegOS models the regulatory world as interconnected business knowledge.

The platform continuously answers questions such as:

- What regulations apply to this product?
- What evidence is still missing?
- Which requirements are satisfied?
- What changed between product versions?
- Which submissions are affected?
- Why was a particular regulatory decision made?

Every answer is traceable through facts, rules, evidence, and historical context.

---

# Core Principles

- Knowledge is the primary asset.
- Facts are the universal language.
- Rules are business knowledge, not application code.
- Decisions must always be explainable.
- Evidence is reusable.
- Products evolve through immutable versions.
- AI assists experts but never replaces accountability.
- Every important business action is traceable.

---

# Platform Overview

RegOS consists of three major platform capabilities.

## Regulatory Knowledge Cloud

Maintains regulatory knowledge including authorities, regulations, requirements, standards, guidance, and the RegOS ontology.

## Regulatory Workspace

Provides each organization with a dedicated workspace containing products, evidence, submissions, decisions, and regulatory history.

## Decision Platform

Transforms trusted knowledge into explainable regulatory decisions using facts, rules, and decision intelligence.

# Repository Structure

This repository follows business architecture rather than technical layers.

Business capabilities are organized into bounded contexts.

Engineering standards, architecture decisions, ontology, and constitutional principles are maintained alongside the source code to ensure long-term consistency.
